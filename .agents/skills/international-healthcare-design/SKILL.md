# INTERNATIONAL HEALTHCARE DESIGN SKILL (ALIVIA STANDARD)

This skill governs all design, layout, typography, art direction, and interaction decisions for the **ALIVIA International Hospital** website and healthcare landing pages.

## Key Principles

1. **Design First, Code Second**: Every layout decision must be grounded in patient psychology (reducing anxiety, establishing trust, enabling fast action).
2. **Quiet Luxury in Healthcare**: Understated clinical authority, generous whitespace (`96px - 140px`), architectural hairlines, and restrained color accents.
3. **Palette Tokens**:
   - Deep Navy (`#0E1E2E` / `#142F43`) - Authority & Primary Actions
   - Warm Ivory (`#FAF9F5` / `#F4F3EE`) - Editorial Canvas & Ambient Comfort
   - Muted Brass / Gold (`#B68B62` / `#A48650`) - JCI Prestige & Quality Highlights
   - Medical Blue (`#1A5F96`) - Precision Navigation & Links
   - Emergency Red (`#C22929`) - 24/7 Hotline & Emergency Access
4. **Editorial Typography**:
   - Display/Headings: `Playfair Display` (Prestige Serif)
   - Body/UI/Forms: `Be Vietnam Pro` (Humanist Sans with pristine Vietnamese diacritics)
5. **Signature International Healthcare Features**:
   - Second Opinion Concierge (Reviewing complex cases with Heart Team / Tumor Board)
   - Dual Care Mode (In-Person Campus Sala vs Telehealth Virtual Care)
   - Guided Symptom Navigator (Routing patients to proper specialties without medical jargon)
   - Insurance Direct Billing Pre-Authorization Check (40+ partners)
   - International Patient & Expat Lounge (Airport pickup, medical translators, visa support)
6. **Conversion Centerpiece (Concierge Scheduling UX)**:
   - 4-step grouped fieldsets (Patient Info -> Medical Need -> Date & Segmented Time -> Privacy Consent under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP)
   - Inline Vietnamese validation & loading states
   - Confirmation Receipt Modal with unique tracking code `ALV-XXXXXX`
